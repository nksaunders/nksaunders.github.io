Dr. Nicholas Saunders · Yale University

Planets across the full
lifetimes of their stars.

I'm a YCAA Prize Postdoctoral Fellow at Yale University studying how planetary systems evolve as their host stars age. I primarily search TESS observations for planets around subgiant and red giant stars, then use Keck to measure their masses and characterize their orbits.
I earned my PhD in Astronomy from the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa in 2025.
